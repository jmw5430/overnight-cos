# Overnight CoS front door: the map

**What it is for.** One word gets the operator the state of their overnight chief of staff and the single next move, without knowing which skill does what.

**The flow.** Settings check, then the three jobs' last runs, then last night's brief, then the open decision count, then one next move.

**Where it branches.** No settings means the answer is "run setup". A failed job leads the answer. Plain-language asks route to the four skills.

**What stays human.** Everything the routed skills say stays human.

**What it never does.** Show a shell command, or offer a menu when one move is clearly next.
