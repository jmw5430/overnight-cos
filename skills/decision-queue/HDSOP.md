# Decision queue: the map

**What it is for.** The sweep can only learn from answers. This turns each item into a five-second tap on the phone and carries the tap back to Notion.

**The flow.** Raise: read each unanswered row, write a plain headline, the real question, a recommended answer with a reason and the draft text, then add the cards to Supersuit's queue. Apply, hourly: write each answer to the log, the task and the rules page, then close the card.

**Where it branches.** Bookkeeping rows never become cards. Sensitive rows hide detail but keep a readable headline. At 15 open cards the queue refuses more until the operator catches up.

**What stays human.** Every tap.

**What it never does.** Send anything, delete a draft or a row, or touch a task beyond its Proposed checkbox or trash.
