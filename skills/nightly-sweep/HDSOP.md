# Nightly sweep: the map

**What it is for.** Nothing the operator owes slips through a day. Every reply owed, promise made and task implied lands in one log and one brief, before they sleep.

**The flow.** Read settings and the rules page, then sweep Gmail, Slack, Notion, calendars and promises in parallel. Write proposed tasks, one CoS Log row per item, and the dated brief.

**Where it branches.** A source that is off or failing is skipped and named in the brief. Anything matching a standing rule or `neverFlag` is skipped and counted. A row already Killed is never raised again.

**What stays human.** Every reply (drafted at 4am, sent by the operator). Accepting any task. Every Response in the log.

**What it never does.** Draft, send, mark anything done, or put sensitive detail in a row or task name.
